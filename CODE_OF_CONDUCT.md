# Code of Conduct

Tokmeter follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1. The short version is below; the linked text is the full one and
governs where they differ.

## Our pledge

We want taking part in Tokmeter to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, whatever their age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good faith. Most bad numbers are bugs, not bad people.
- Disagree about code with evidence, not with the person.
- Accept feedback on your work gracefully, and give it the same way.
- Own your mistakes, fix them, and learn from them.

## What is not acceptable

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insults, and personal or political attacks.
- Harassment, in public or in private.
- Publishing someone's private information (an address, an email, the
  contents of their session logs) without their explicit permission.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions,
reviews) and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report anything that breaks this code to the maintainer at
**hello@srinivas.dev**. Reports are read only by the maintainer and kept
confidential. You'll get a reply within a few days.

## Enforcement

The maintainer decides on the response, from a private warning, through a
temporary ban from project spaces, to a permanent one, following the
[Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
